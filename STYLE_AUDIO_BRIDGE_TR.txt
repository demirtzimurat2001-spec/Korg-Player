## Follow-up implementation: playable STG selection and melodic fallback (25 Oct 2026)
- Added a UI action to choose among up to 120 STG files whose embedded MIDI can actually be parsed, instead of silently forcing the first archive entry. Selection updates the active container/sequence used by Variation playback and named-scene extraction.
- Internal audio now tracks MIDI program-change messages per channel. For melodic STG notes where no decoded Korg sample zone is available, the engine synthesizes a simple program-dependent fallback voice; percussion channel 10 continues to prefer the dedicated built-in drum voices. This makes more parsed notes audible in the phone mixer, but it is not a Korg sound-bank emulator and is not original SET PCM.
- Parser regression check on the supplied STG sample still reports: Intro2 156 note-ons, Var1 577, Var2 1,640, Var3 184, Var4 299, Break 42. Missing Fill/Ending branches remain missing in that file; the app must not claim otherwise.
- Android build and device audio output remain unverified in this environment because Android SDK/Gradle are unavailable. No APK is claimed.
